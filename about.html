Our Story & Origins

Rooted in Ganjam, Odisha • Serving Global Markets

Padmasri Global Exports (operating in synergy with Padmasri Enterprises) was founded 7 to 8 years ago with a single mission: to provide international buyers direct, verified access to India's most precious natural and cold-chain commodities.

Based in Govindapur, P.O: Surula, Block: Chikiti, Ganjam - 761009, Odisha, our location is uniquely strategic. Ganjam district is the world epicenter of Kewda Flower (Pandanus fascicularis) distillation, accounting for nearly 90% of global Kewda production with an official Geographical Indication (GI) tag.

Simultaneously, our close proximity to Odisha's 480km Bay of Bengal coastline and leading commercial ports (Paradip and Visakhapatnam) enables us to deliver blast-frozen seafood and Halal poultry under uninterrupted cold-chain discipline.

Operational History

7 to 8 Years of International Growth Milestones

Tracing our evolutionary roadmap from local procurement in Ganjam to multi-continental trade lanes.

2018 – FOUNDATION

Direct Farm & Distillation Network

Established direct partnerships with traditional copper deg-bhapka artisan distillers across Chikiti and the Ganjam coastal belt, creating ethical supply chains for 100% pure Kewda Ruh.

2020 – COLD-CHAIN EXPANSION

-40°C Cryogenic Blast Infrastructure

Invested in cryogenic blast tunnels and temperature-monitored refrigerated transport for commercial poultry and seafood, preventing thermal degradation.

2022 – STATUTORY ACCREDITATIONS

APEDA, Halal & MPEDA Approvals

Secured statutory trade licenses and 100% Halal certification, unlocking high-volume poultry and marine shipments to the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia.

2024–2026 – GLOBAL CORRIDORS

Deep-Port Berth Allocations

Formed direct priority handling agreements at Paradip Port (PPT) and Visakhapatnam Port (VPT), routing 40ft High-Cube reefer containers to Southeast Asia, Europe, and the Middle East.
