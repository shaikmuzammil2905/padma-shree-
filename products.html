Pure Ganjam Kewda Ruh (Pandanus fascicularis)

The world's most prized natural screwpine aromatic essence, harvested and hydro-distilled in traditional copper deg-bhapkas across the coastal belt of Chikiti, Ganjam, Odisha.

Kewda Ruh is obtained from the male inflorescence of the Pandanus fascicularis plant. Over 85-90% of the world's supply originates exclusively from this specific coastal terroir of Ganjam district, recognized with an official Geographical Indication (GI) tag.

Technical Specifications

Botanical Source: Pandanus fascicularis
CAS Number: 93455-95-9
Extraction Method: Traditional Hydro-distillation
Specific Gravity: 0.920 – 0.950 at 25°C
Refractive Index: 1.485 – 1.515 at 20°C
Appearance: Pale Yellow to Golden Liquid
