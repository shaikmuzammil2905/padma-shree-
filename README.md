## 🌿 Core Commodities & Export Sectors

1. **Pure Ganjam Kewda Ruh & Floral Extracts (GI Tag)**
   - 100% pure hydro-distilled *Pandanus fascicularis* extract produced in traditional copper degs & bhapkas.
   - Sourced directly from the coastal belt of Chikiti, Ganjam, Odisha (world capital of Kewda flower distillation).
   - CAS: 93455-95-9. Food-grade aluminum canister & UN barrel packaging.
2. **Grade-A Certified Frozen Halal Chicken**
   - 100% Halal manual slaughter, blast frozen at -38°C and maintained at -18°C.
   - Whole griller chickens (800g - 1400g calibrated) and individual portions (boneless breast fillets, drumsticks, wings).
   - ISO 22000 & HACCP compliant.
3. **Pristine Bay of Bengal Marine Seafood**
   - Black Tiger Shrimp (*Penaeus monodon*), Vannamei White Shrimp, Sea Bass (Bhetki), Ribbon Fish, and Pomfret.
   - Block frozen & cryogenic IQF; APEDA & MPEDA registered.
4. **End-to-End Cold-Chain Logistics**
   - 20ft & 40ft High-Cube Reefer Containers with real-time temperature loggers.
   - Strategic dispatch from Paradip Port (Odisha) and Visakhapatnam Port (Andhra Pradesh) to major worldwide destination ports (Jebel Ali, Singapore, Port Klang, Rotterdam, New York).
