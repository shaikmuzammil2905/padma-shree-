About Padmasri Global Exports

A Heritage of Purity, Cold-Chain Mastery & Global Trade Dependability

Headquartered in Chikiti Block, Ganjam District, Odisha, Padmasri Global Exports & Enterprises bridges local agricultural wealth and pristine marine reserves with international buyers.

Logistics & Infrastructure

Zero-Break Cold Chain From Factory Gates to Overseas Docks

Perishable commodities require uncompromising temperature discipline. Our integrated supply chain guarantees unbroken preservation at every milestone.

1

Coastal Harvesting & Immediate Processing

Kewra flowers are hydro-distilled within 6 hours of morning plucking. Seafood and poultry are blast frozen at -38°C immediately following humane Halal slaughter and coastal landing.

2

Lab Testing & Microbiological Validation

Every batch is checked for Salmonella, E.coli, moisture, chemical residues, and GC-MS purity in certified analytical laboratories.

3

Food-Grade Export Packaging

Heavy wax-coated cartons with moisture-proof LDPE food-grade liners, sealed aluminum canisters for Kewda Ruh, and shrink-wrapped pallets.

4

GPS & Telemetry Monitored Reefer Shipping

Transit via Daikin / Carrier reefer containers maintaining constant -18°C with digital data loggers loaded at Paradip and Visakhapatnam deep sea ports.

Key Gateway Sea Ports

Strategically located between Paradip Port (Odisha) and Visakhapatnam Port (Andhra Pradesh), ensuring the fastest transit feeder connections to Colombo, Singapore, and Jebel Ali.
